CONCIERGE DESK

Frequently Asked Questions

Essential guidance regarding private fittings, tailoring turnaround, international fabric mills, and sartorial care.

While walk-in clients are warmly welcomed at our flagship boutique during showroom hours, we recommend reserving a private styling appointment to guarantee dedicated time with our Master Outfitter. Private sessions include complimentary collar and cuff measure-up, cloth swatch review, and bespoke beverage service.

Minor adjustments such as trouser hems and sleeve taper adjustments are completed within 48 to 72 hours. Comprehensive alterations, including shoulder resets or full-canvas jacket suppression, require 5 to 7 business days by our in-house master tailors to preserve the hand-basted drape.

Yes. Every product card and shopping bag order incorporates direct one-click WhatsApp Concierge functionality. Simply tap "WhatsApp Enquiry" or "Enquire via WhatsApp" to share your garment selection with our senior clothier, who will verify your neck, chest, and inseam measurements prior to dispatch.

We exclusively partner with historic European mills: Albini and Thomas Mason for 2-ply 100s–140s Egyptian Giza and Sea Island cottons, Vitale Barberis Canonico and Reda for Super 120s–150s Australian virgin merino wools, and Como heritage ateliers for pure mulberry jacquard silk ties.

We offer a 30-day sartorial satisfaction policy on all unworn, unaltered catalogue garments. If the fit does not meet your discerning expectations, we provide complimentary size swaps or atelier adjustments at our flagship boutique.

Yes. Our Executive Services Division caters to boardroom suites, legal chambers, and wedding parties. We offer on-site fitting sessions for executive leadership teams and bespoke group coordination for black-tie galas and ceremonies.
