THE FOUR PILLARS

What Defines VESTARO

An uncompromising standard across construction, cloth, silhouette, and longevity.

Refined Tailoring

Floating horsehair chest canvases, hand-rolled lapels, and working surgeon's cuffs crafted with 22 stitches per inch for enduring structure.

Premium Fabrics

Sourced from venerable European and Egyptian mills: Super 150s merino wool, cavalry twill, two-ply Royal Oxford cotton, and seven-fold Como silk.

Exceptional Fit

Engineered across Slim Fit and Regular Fit blocks with high armholes and tapered waists that flatter without restricting natural movement.

Timeless Design

Balanced lapel proportions, classic spread collars, and a disciplined palette of midnight black, charcoal, navy, and warm ivory that transcends seasons.
