SARTORIAL DIALOGUE

Brand Storytelling & Dress Codes

Understanding how our six house brands converse across classic heritage and contemporary silhouettes.

01 • SILHOUETTE DIALOGUE

Classic vs Contemporary

Where Classic Tailors honours generous Jermyn Street Regular Fit proportions and pleated fronts, Urban Gentleman sculpts closer to the frame with high armholes, narrow lapels, and flat-front tapered trousers.

02 • FIVE-DAY PERFORMANCE

Everyday Businesswear

Modern Office Collection and The Executive Edit prioritize high-twist crease recovery, non-iron two-ply cotton finishes, and internal silicone shirt-grips that keep you looking pressed through long commutes and flights.

03 • CEREMONIAL GRANDEUR

Premium Occasionwear

For black-tie galas, milestone weddings, and diplomatic banquets, VESTARO Signature and Heritage Formal unite Super 150s three-piece tailoring with horseshoe waistcoats and seven-fold Como silk ties.

04 • VERSATILE SEPARATES

Modern Professional Style

Pair an unstructured hopsack blazer from VESTARO Signature with contrasting charcoal or ivory Gurkha trousers for a refined smart-formal look that bridges creative leadership and traditional authority.
